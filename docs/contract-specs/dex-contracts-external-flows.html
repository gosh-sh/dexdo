Full HTML documentation for the stateful externally reachable entrypoints in `contracts/`. The focus here is not the contract structure as such but which runtime flows the external calls trigger, which callbacks they spawn, and where external events appear.

The diagrams are inline SVG with no external JS, so rendering is predictable locally. Red badges on the sequence diagrams are external `emit ...{dest: ...}` events; the destination address is written directly inside each event block, with no separate tables or captions below the diagrams.

1. Bootstrap, Voucher, Fee Top-Up

This chain covers three separate flows: `PrivateNote` creation, shell-fee top-up via `Nullifier`, and voucher-event generation in `RootPN`.

2. Oracle Deployment And Registry

The Oracle branch consists of `RootOracle`, `Oracle`, and `OracleEventList`. `OracleEventList` is what stores the `EventInfo` records and confirms the market for PMP.

3. Market Deployment, Oracle Approval, Freeze

`PrivateNote` creates the `PMP`, the `PMP` collects approvals from every `OracleEventList`, records the deployer's initial stakes on the first approval, then transitions into approved state, receives timings, and only after freezing brings up `OrderBook`.

4. Oracle Governance Entry Flows

After approval the market is governed by quorum mechanics inside `PMP`. The entry points here are not `private`: external messages into `submitSetTimings`, `submitResolve`, and `submitCancelEvent` collect votes from oracle signers or trusted addresses and only then flip the market's main state.

5. Stake, Split, Merge, Resolve, Claim

This is the main market execution path after approval: regular stake, full-set split/merge, then resolve and claim. The mirrored PMP/PrivateNote event pairs are most visible here.

6. OrderBook: Placement And Fill

The order flow is split into a queue and callbacks. In the single-order path, `PrivateNote` first emits `OrderSubmitted` and only then receives `OrderBook`'s callbacks via `OrderPlacedConfirmed` / `OrderFilledConfirmed`. Batch placement reuses the same queue/match machinery without an `OrderSubmitted` per item, terminated by `onBatchComplete(...)`.

7. OrderBook: Cancel, Reject, Bounce

Cancellation, reject, and callback-bounce form a separate observability path. The service events `Queued`, `Rejected`, and `CallbackBounced` are what matters here. For the batch cancel path `cancelBatch()` / `cancelAllOrders()`, `OrderBook` also terminates the series with the `onBatchComplete(...)` callback without a dedicated event object.

8. PrivateNote To PrivateNote Transfer

P2P transfer lives apart from the order/stake machinery: the sender locks the amount, the receiver immediately credits the balance, then sends the ack.

9. Wallet Withdraw, Coupon, Local Admin, Recovery

Not all externally reachable entrypoints lead into the big market flow. `PrivateNote`, `RootPN`, and the root contracts have separate wallet and administrative branches: withdraw, transfer recovery, owner rotation, coupon issuance, and code upgrade.

10. Terminal Shutdown And Last Claim Tail

The final claim is not just the last winner's payout. If `_totalWinPool == 0` after it, `PMP` closes the market: sends residual to the deployer, triggers `OrderBook.shutdown()`, and itself goes into `selfdestruct`.

11. Internal Callbacks With State Changes

Not every important transition in the system has an external event object. Below are the internal callbacks that actually mutate contract state and therefore matter when reading flows alongside the sequence diagrams.

Where it fires Internal callback What it changes Adjacent external event
PMP first `approveEvent()` `PrivateNote.onInitialStakesAccepted(...)` Records the deployer's initial stakes across the outcome arrays and clears the initial indeterminacy of `_busy` on the creator note. `ApprovedByOracle` may follow later in the same approval lifecycle, but only on the final oracle.
PMP reject / invalid initial stakes `PrivateNote.onInitialStakesFailed(...)` Refunds the deployer's initial stakes and unfreezes the note when the market fails to initialize. Typically followed by `PMPRejected`, but the callback itself has no event object.
PMP `_ensureFrozen()` `PrivateNote.onPmpCleanRefund(...)` Refunds the creator the clean-pool remainders before the integer split/merge basket is built. `PoolsFrozen` follows.
OrderBook reject place-path `PrivateNote.onOrderRejected(...)` Restores the buy-lock or sell-lock when an order is rejected during pre-validation or because of an overflowed queue. `Rejected` is emitted on the `OrderBook` side.
OrderBook batch place / cancel / cancel-all `PrivateNote.onBatchComplete(...)` Clears the `_pendingBatch*` guard and `_busy` after the entire batch has been dispatched through callbacks. No dedicated event object; the batch reuses the regular `Queued` / `OrderPlaced` / `OrderCancelled` / `OrderFilled` along the way.
